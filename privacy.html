Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version. This site sets no cookies, has no accounts, forms or sign-ups, and collects nothing that identifies you. It uses one cookie-free visitor counter that we run ourselves.

Who runs this site

Stuff Diver is run by Dan Eve, trading as CryptoRaptor (the “controller” of any personal data described here). You can reach us at info@leveluplogic.co.uk.

What we record

We run our own copy of Umami, open-source analytics, to count visitors. It does not use cookies or local storage. It records the pages viewed, the referring site, your browser, operating system, device type and approximate location (country). Your IP address is used to work out the country and to make a short-lived anonymous visitor code, and is not stored. We only see totals, and we do not know who you are. We use this to see which pages are useful. Because it is not tied to you, you do not need to consent to it, and you can block it with any ad or tracker blocker.

Hosting and third parties

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any host, can log your IP address and request details for security and operation; GitHub’s privacy statement applies. The download buttons ask GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release, so GitHub sees your IP address when you use them; GitHub's privacy statement applies. Fonts and images are served from this site.

Stuff Diver itself is a desktop app that runs on your computer; this page covers only the website.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be erased, or object to its use. Because the visitor counts are anonymous totals, we normally cannot link any of them to you, but ask us if in doubt. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this changes we will update the date at the top.
